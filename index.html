Voxel Wrecker: Loot Hunt

Gold bars (+1), gems (+2) and a flag (+3) are hidden around the map: inside crates (some are empty), sealed rooms and other secret spots. Smash your way in, walk over the loot to grab it.

The detector gets hotter (and clicks faster) as you near the closest loot. Collapses and blasts send loot flying. No time limit: the clock runs until you've found everything. Fastest times go on the leaderboard.
